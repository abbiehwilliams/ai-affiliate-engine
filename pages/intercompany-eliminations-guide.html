How to Set Up Intercompany Eliminations Guide for Multi-Entity Financial Consolidation

Solution Summary: Connect multiple accounting organizations (Xero, QuickBooks Online, Sage) into a single unified workspace using Intercompany Eliminations Guide. This workflow automates intercompany eliminations, multi-currency conversions, and consolidated P&L reporting.

Prerequisites

  • Active admin credentials for your accounting platform (Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, or spreadsheet files).
  • An active account or free trial via the Official Intercompany Eliminations Guide Platform Portal.

Step-by-Step Setup Instructions

1 Launch the Portal & Initialize Workspace

Navigate to the registration page and log in to your account. Click on Create New Workspace from your main user dashboard.

📸 [Image Placeholder: Dashboard Workspace Creation Screen]
Visual: Main dashboard view showing the "Add Companies / Create Workspace" button.

2 Connect Primary Accounting Entities

Click Companies on the left navigation menu, then select Add Company. Choose your primary software vendor (e.g., Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage) and complete the single-sign-on permission workflow.

📸 [Image Placeholder: Accounting Integration Selection Screen]
Visual: Integration grid displaying Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, and Excel connection prompts.

3 Map Chart of Accounts & Set Exchange Rates

For organizations operating across currencies, configure your base reporting currency. The platform automatically pulls daily exchange rates and maps mismatched general ledger (GL) accounts across parent and child entities.

📸 [Image Placeholder: Chart of Accounts & Currency Mapping Console]
Visual: Account mapping table matching child entity accounts to the group parent structure.

4 Run Consolidated Reports & Export Board Packs

Navigate to the Reports tab. Select Consolidated Profit & Loss or Consolidated Balance Sheet. Apply intercompany balance eliminations with one click and export as a PDF board pack or Excel file.

📸 [Image Placeholder: Final Consolidated Financial Report View]
Visual: Clean multi-entity financial statement showing group totals and eliminations.
